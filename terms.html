2. Student & Participant Conduct

Students and workshop attendees are expected to uphold the highest standards of safety, ethics, and mutual respect:

  • Hardware Safety: Adhere strictly to laboratory protocols regarding soldering irons, lithium-polymer (LiPo) batteries, high-speed drone rotors, and 3D printing thermal extruders.
  • Academic Integrity: All capstone code, robotics designs, and project submissions must represent the student's authentic work.
  • No Reverse Engineering: Proprietary firmware, board schematics, and specialized curriculum materials are provided strictly for educational usage.

3. Institutional Turnkey Lab Setup Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

For engineering colleges, universities, and schools commissioning turnkey Robotics, AI, IoT, and Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL):

  • Hardware Delivery & Calibration: Robotree guarantees certified hardware delivery, bench installation, and safety grounding within the agreed contract timeline (typically 14–30 calendar days).
  • Warranty & Replacement: All core microcontroller boards, sensors, and 3D printers include a 12-month manufacturer replacement warranty against factory defects.
  • Faculty Development Programs (FDP): Each lab setup includes mandatory certified master trainer sessions for resident professors and STEM mentors.

4. Certificate Issuance & Verification Integrity

Official Robotree Certificates are tamper-proof academic credentials issued under ISO 9001:2015 certified standards:

  • Each certificate contains a cryptographically unique serial identifier verifiable 24/7 at robotree.org/certificate.html.
  • Any attempt to forge, modify, or misrepresent certificate credentials will result in immediate permanent revocation and academic notification to the affiliated institution.

5. Pricing, Payments & Refund Policy

Transparent pricing is enforced across our educational tiers:

  • Course Fees: Course fees must be remitted prior to hardware kit dispatch or batch orientation.
  • Cancellations: Written cancellation requests received at least 7 calendar days before course commencement are eligible for a 100% refund (less standard payment gateway processing charges).
  • Non-Refundable Phase: Once hardware development kits have been unsealed and dispatched to the student's residential address, kit materials are non-refundable.
