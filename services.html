4 Integrated Technology Tracks in Every RJIL Lab

Every partner school receives complete hardware, software simulators, tools, and hands-on inventory across four future-ready disciplines:

Track 01 • Hardware & Coding

Robotics & ATL Innovation

Hands-on microcontrollers, sensor integration, autonomous bots, robo-soccer, circuit prototyping, and computational logic.

  • Microcontroller kits (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi)
  • Sensor fusion: ultrasonic, IR, line sensors, gyro
  • Motor driver shields, robotic arms, smart rovers
  • Block coding (Scratch) to C/C++ embedded firmware
Track 02 • Aerodynamics

Drone Technology Lab

Aerodynamic engineering, quadcopter frame mechanics, flight physics, safety cages, and computer-based drone flight simulators.

  • Quadcopter structural assembly & calibration
  • Brushless DC motors, ESCs, LiPo power safety
  • Virtual flight simulator software & flight rigs
  • Autonomous obstacle avoidance & aerial navigation
Track 03 • Additive Manufacturing

3D Printing & Design Lab

CAD modeling, 3D slicing, filament extrusion, and real-time rapid prototyping turning digital student ideas into physical functional objects.

  • Industrial-grade FDM 3D printing equipment
  • Tinkercad, Autodesk Fusion 360 & Cura slicing
  • Eco-friendly PLA filament stocks & maintenance tools
  • Rapid prototyping for science fair and robotics chassis
Track 04 • Immersive Learning

AR / VR Immersive Lab

7 Dedicated Virtual Reality Headsets loaded with interactive 3D STEM content, virtual science laboratories, space exploration, and anatomical simulations.

  • 7 High-immersion VR Headset units deployed
  • Interactive 3D physics, chemistry & biology labs
  • Virtual walkthroughs of planetary systems & history
  • Safe hands-on simulation of high-voltage & chemical experiments

Shared Responsibilities Framework

Clear division of operational roles ensuring frictionless day-to-day lab execution:

Delivered by Robotree Robotics LLP

  • 2 Dedicated Full-Time Trainers: Qualified robotics and STEM educators permanently stationed at the school.
  • Complete Hardware & Tools: 100% equipment supply, 3D printers, VR headsets, drone components and robotics kits.
  • Structured Curriculum: NEP 2020-compliant lesson plans and graded courseware for Classes 3 through 12.
  • Quarterly Servicing & Replacements: Free repair, maintenance, damaged component replacement and firmware updates.
  • Competitions & Hackathons: Organizing intra-school exhibitions, regional championships, and national contest entries.
  • Portfolios & Verifiable Certificates: Continuous student assessments, project journals, and verifiable digital certificates.

Provided by School Administration

  • Dedicated Lab Space: An allocated room of approximately 500 – 800 sq. ft. for exclusive RJIL activities.
  • Basic Utilities: Standard 230V uninterrupted electricity supply, adequate illumination, and broadband internet.
  • Timetable Alignment: Allocation of regular school timetable periods or structured after-school batches.
  • Internal Coordinator: 1 designated teacher coordinator to interface with the Robotree trainer team.
  • Student Enrollment Support: Encouraging student participation and collecting monthly subscription dues.
