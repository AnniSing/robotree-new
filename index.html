Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Robotree Junior Innovation Labs (RJIL), school setups, and hands-on courses.

What is the Robotree Junior Innovation Lab (RJIL)?

Robotree Junior Innovation Lab (RJIL) is a turnkey STEM and future-skills laboratory setup for schools (Grades 1 to 12). It brings hands-on Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Drones, 3D Printing, IoT, and AR/VR directly into the school curriculum, fully aligned with NEP 2020 guidelines.

Which school grades and age groups are eligible for RJIL programs?

RJIL programs are tailored for students aged 6 to 18 (Grades 1 through 12). We offer age-stratified learning tiers: foundational visual block coding for primary students, mechanical assembly and sensor integration for middle school, and industrial Python, computer vision, and autonomous drone flight algorithms for senior students.

Does Robotree provide turnkey equipment, 3D printers, and trainer support?

Yes, completely end-to-end. We supply high-precision 3D printers, student robotics toolkits, quadcopters, microcontrollers, sensor arrays, workstation layouts, and certified resident or visiting STEM trainers who manage classes and mentor students for national competitions throughout the academic session.

Are Robotree certificates recognized by academic and industry partners?

Yes. Every student who successfully completes a Robotree curriculum track or workshop earns a verifiable certificate featuring an immutable digital Verification ID. Institutions and universities can independently verify authenticity 24/7 on our Certificate Verification Portal.

How can our school, college, or university schedule a live demo or setup consultation?

Schools can easily request a consultation by filling out our Institutional Inquiry Form or speaking directly with our Lab Architecture team via WhatsApp at +91 7979038537. We will prepare a customized space design, kit roster, and budget estimate tailored to your student cohort.
