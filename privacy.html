Legal & Data Governance

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 ? Last Reviewed: August 28, 2026

At Robotree, we are committed to transparent, ethical, and legally compliant data handling in compliance with GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (US), and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India).

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Robotree ("we", "us", "our") operates the educational robotics platform, training academies, and turnkey STEM/ATL lab setups reachable at robotree.org. We act as the Data Controller for personal information collected via our website, course registrations, certificate verification portal, and institutional partnership inquiries.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to deliver our educational programs, authenticate certificates, and maintain account security:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, email address, phone number, school/college affiliation, and student ID.
  • Academic & Certification Data: Course enrollments, workshop attendance, project evaluation scores, and unique certificate identifiers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type/version, operating system, request timestamps, and anonymized performance telemetry.
  • Authentication Data: Cryptographically hashed passwords (salted PBKDF2 with 210,000 rounds) and encrypted session tokens. We never store plain-text passwords.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process your personal information under the following recognized legal bases:

  • Performance of Contract: To deliver robotics courses, provide lab hardware kits, and issue verified completion credentials.
  • Legitimate Interests: To detect security threats, prevent unauthorized access, mitigate brute-force attempts, and improve curriculum quality.
  • Legal Obligation: To maintain verifiable academic records, financial accounting, and tax compliance.
  • Explicit Consent: For marketing newsletters, non-essential analytics cookies, and voluntary event testimonials.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR, CCPA & DPDP Act

Every user and student has comprehensive privacy rights over their personal data:

Right to Access Export your complete data dossier via GET /api/auth/export-data.
Right to Erasure Permanently delete your account and profile via POST /api/auth/delete-account.
Right to Object Opt out of non-essential cookies and marketing communications at any time.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & DPDP Act Compliance)

Robotree provides junior robotics programs for school students. When enrolling students under 18 years of age, parental or institutional consent is mandatory. We strictly prohibit behavioral profiling, advertising targeting, or data monetization of minors.

6. Cookie & Tracking Technologies

We use a privacy-first cookie policy:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: robotree_csrf (Anti-CSRF protection) and robotree_session (Authentication token). These cookies are essential for security and cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: robotree_theme (Dark/Light mode preference).
  • Telemetry Cookies: Anonymized conversion telemetry (only activated upon clicking "Accept All Cookies").

7. Data Retention & Security Standards

We implement defense-in-depth security architectures to protect all data:

  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 encryption across all client-server communications with HSTS preloading.
  • Encryption at Rest: Cryptographic hashing and tokenization for passwords and administrative keys.
  • Retention Schedule: Inactive account data is pruned after 36 months unless academic certification retention is mandated.

8. Contact Our Data Protection Officer (DPO)

For any privacy requests, data export inquiries, or grievance redressal, please contact our designated Data Protection Officer:

Data Protection Officer: Robotree Legal & Compliance Team

Email: privacy@robotree.org / support@robotree.org

Corporate Address: Robotree Academy & Research Lab, India

Response Timeline: We respond to all verified data subject requests within 30 calendar days.
